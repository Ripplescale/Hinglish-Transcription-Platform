# oats 0.7.4

Prepared 7 October 2026 for the configured Windows development laptop.

Repeated Trelis text can replace real speech at a chunk boundary. This update
first retries with surrounding audio, using up to 30 seconds of saved sound.
During capture it waits for the needed following audio while retaining the
first text as provisional. A successful retry replaces the entire covered
range; untouched neighboring portions are transcribed separately. It does
not guess which words belong to an overlap.

If that attempt fails, is empty, still loops, reaches the generation limit,
or cannot preserve neighboring speech, the original window gets a bounded
fallback in pieces of at most 5 seconds. A completed, nonempty combined
fallback becomes the displayed and copied transcript, even if repetition
remains. Failed or entirely empty fallbacks retain the original. All attempts
and superseded recognition remain available for review.

Live and saved transcripts now use chat bubbles: computer audio on the left,
microphone audio on the right. The source labels do not identify individual
speakers. Existing mixed Hindi/English text is preserved. Recovery details,
original text and earlier corrections remain inspectable. An open correction
draft survives replacement and can be explicitly applied as an earlier-text
reference or cancelled; it is never transferred to new recognition.

The Python speech gate now supports the verified Silero 6.2.3 ONNX model,
with 512-sample frames and the modern model's 64-sample context. Microphone
and computer tracks are checked separately. The conservative threshold and
analysis gain policy are retained. Amplification limits were studied
separately and are not a deployed silence filter. The waveform sent to ASR
is unchanged.

Generation diagnostics record token count, an observed end marker,
length-limit status and UTF-8/zlib text compression. The observer never
changes token scores or stops generation. Confidence and no-speech scores
remain unavailable; they are not used to delete speech. Soft repetition
penalties, a hard three-repeat rule and denoising remain disabled.

## Deployment

The desktop app and worker must be updated together. Worker candidates may
be staged without activation. Save notes and fully quit through the tray
before installing and activating the paired update. Original recordings,
saved notes and correction history are preserved.

The unsigned installer reuses the laptop's separately prepared local models
and Python environments. It is not qualified for a clean machine or general
hardware distribution. Models, runtime pointers, private recordings and
experimental transcripts are excluded from GitHub.

## Validation

Targeted checks cover bounded recovery, overlap preservation, interruption
and restart, fallback selection, revision-aware imports, raw-text history,
earlier correction validation, speech-gate provisioning, and selected-text
copy/export. Interface checks use fictional data and include narrow layouts,
live replacement, saved corrections and retained drafts.

Nine fresh GPU inference calls verified three known real-speech failures
through the updated recovery helper. All three selected the previously
recovered text exactly, retained the remaining neighboring range without
overlap or gaps, and archived both replaced originals. The diagnostic
observer preserved baseline and context output exactly. These development
checks do not establish general accuracy, guarantee every missing word will
be recovered, or prove that any chunk size is optimal. Silent regions can
still produce hallucinations. Recovery adds processing time and may delay
the affected track. Physical-call and installed-upgrade checks remain
separate deployment validation.
